What We Do

Practice Areas

Nine areas of practice, and the matters the chamber handles in each, from its office in Upper Gizri, Karachi.

01

Civil Law

Property disputes, injunctions, recovery suits, contracts and damages claims, handled with careful drafting and steady court follow-through.

  • Property and possession disputes
  • Permanent and temporary injunctions
  • Recovery and damages suits
  • Breach of contract claims
02

Criminal Law

Bail, trial defence and quashment matters, argued on the record and on the law, from the magistrate's court to the High Court.

  • Bail and pre-arrest bail petitions
  • Trial defence and cross-examination
  • Quashment and revision petitions
  • Appeals against conviction or acquittal
03

Family Law

Khula, dissolution, maintenance, custody and inheritance matters handled with discretion and a focus on lawful, workable outcomes.

  • Khula and dissolution of marriage
  • Maintenance for wife and children
  • Custody and visitation of minors
  • Inheritance and succession matters
04

Banking Law

Banking court matters, loan recovery, finance disputes and documentation for borrowers and financial institutions alike.

  • Banking court suits and defence
  • Loan recovery and restructuring
  • Finance documentation review
  • Disputes with financial institutions
05

Corporate Law

Company formation, regulatory compliance, commercial contracts and day-to-day legal support for businesses in Pakistan.

  • Company incorporation and SECP matters
  • Shareholder and director disputes
  • Commercial contracts and agreements
  • Regulatory compliance and notices
06

Revenue Law

Tax and revenue matters, assessments, appeals and representation before revenue authorities and tribunals.

  • Income tax assessments and appeals
  • Sales tax matters and notices
  • Representation before tribunals
  • Reply to departmental notices
07

Rent Law

Eviction, rent fixation and landlord-tenant disputes under the applicable rent laws, for both owners and occupants.

  • Eviction proceedings
  • Rent fixation and enhancement
  • Recovery of rent arrears
  • Tenancy agreements and notices
08

Insurance Law

Insurance claims, repudiation disputes and policy matters, pursued through negotiation or the courts as the case requires.

  • Repudiated insurance claims
  • Policy interpretation disputes
  • Claims against insurers
  • Consumer and court proceedings
09

Labour Law

Employment disputes, service matters, wages and termination cases for employees, workers and employers.

  • Termination and dismissal disputes
  • Wages and dues recovery
  • Service matters and inquiries
  • Labour court representation

Do not see your exact matter listed? Bring the documents to the chamber; if it falls within the firm's practice, you will be told so plainly, and if it does not, you will be guided to the right forum.
