What We Do

Practice Areas

Nine areas of practice, one standard of work: verified law, careful drafting and honest counsel.

01

Civil Law

Property disputes, injunctions, recovery suits, contracts and damages claims handled with careful drafting and steady court follow-through.

02

Criminal Law

Bail, trial defence and quashment matters, argued on the record and on the law, from the magistrate's court to the High Court.

03

Family Law

Khula, dissolution, maintenance, custody and inheritance matters handled with discretion and a focus on lawful, workable outcomes.

04

Banking Law

Banking court matters, loan recovery, finance disputes and documentation for borrowers and financial institutions alike.

05

Corporate Law

Company formation, regulatory compliance, commercial contracts and day-to-day legal support for businesses in Pakistan.

06

Revenue Law

Tax and revenue matters, assessments, appeals and representation before revenue authorities and tribunals.

07

Rent Law

Eviction, rent fixation and landlord-tenant disputes under the applicable rent laws, for both owners and occupants.

08

Insurance Law

Insurance claims, repudiation disputes and policy matters, pursued through negotiation or the courts as the case requires.

09

Labour Law

Employment disputes, service matters, wages and termination cases for employees, workers and employers.
