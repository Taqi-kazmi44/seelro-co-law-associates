Who We Are

Counsel for individuals, families and businesses

Seelro & Co. Law Associates is a Karachi law firm working across civil, criminal, family, banking, corporate, revenue, rent, insurance and labour law. The chamber is led by its Managing Partner, Adv. Saifullah Magsi, with Adv. Farhan Khan Seelro, Advocate High Court, at the centre of its court practice.

Clients come to the firm with property disputes, criminal charges, family matters, business questions and claims that cannot wait. The firm's answer is always the same discipline: read everything, verify the law, and proceed on a plan the client understands.

How We Work
  • First, we listen. The full facts, in the client's own words.
  • Then, we verify. Every position checked against the current law.
  • Then, we advise in writing. Options, risks and costs, plainly stated.
  • Then, we act. Notice, filing or negotiation, as the matter requires.
