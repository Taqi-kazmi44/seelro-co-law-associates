Common Questions

Before you visit

How do I book a consultation?
Call or WhatsApp the chamber on 0301-3530007, or send an email describing your matter in brief. You will be given an appointment at the Upper Gizri office.
What documents should I bring to the first meeting?
Bring your CNIC and every paper connected with the matter: court notices, agreements, receipts, correspondence and any earlier orders. The more complete the file, the more accurate the first assessment.
What will the legal fees be?
Fees depend on the nature and stage of the matter. They are discussed openly and agreed with you after the first assessment, before any filing is made. There are no hidden charges.
Which courts does the firm appear before?
The chamber appears before the High Court and the subordinate courts, including district courts, banking courts, consumer courts and tribunals, according to the nature of the matter.
Will I be kept informed about my case?
Yes. After every hearing you are told what happened, what was ordered and what the next date is, so your matter never moves without your knowledge.
Does the firm send legal notices?
Yes. Where a matter can be resolved without litigation, the chamber drafts and serves a formal legal notice first, and proceeds to court only if the notice does not settle the matter.
